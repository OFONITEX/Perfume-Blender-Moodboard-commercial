Visual Reference Board

Bottle Silhouette

1. Fluted Glass Geometry & Proportions

Architectural ribbed fluting running vertically down the bottle walls with a heavy weighted glass base.

Blender Workflow:
  • Cylinder primitive with 24-32 vertical loop cuts.
  • Checker deselect → scale inward with Bevel (`Ctrl + B`).
  • Solidify modifier + Subdivision Surface (`Ctrl + 2`).
Materials & Finishes

2. Knurled Brass Atomizer & Collar

Tactile machined knurling texture with micro-scratches, giving the product an authentic luxury weight.

Blender Workflow:
  • Principled BSDF: Metallic `1.0`, Roughness `0.18–0.24`.
  • Add procedural knurled bump map (Diamond Voronoi or image texture).
  • Subtle Anisotropic shading for circular lathe reflections.
Lighting & Caustics

3. Radiant Amber Backlight & Dispersion

Light rays projecting through the glass and fluid, forming luminous geometric web caustics across the floor.

Blender Workflow:
  • Enable Cycles "Shadow Caustics" on bottle, liquid, and floor.
  • Narrow beam Spot Light positioned behind the bottle pointing forward.
  • Volume Scatter in World Shader (`Density: 0.005`) for light rays.
Environment & Staging

4. Minimalist Obsidian Pedestal

Cylindrical dark Nero Marquina marble podium with brass inlay ring. Subtle ground reflections and ambient smoke.

Blender Workflow:
  • Beveled cylinder with inset loop cuts for brass metal bands.
  • PBR Marble texture (Color, Roughness, Normal) from Poly Haven.
  • Overhead softbox light casting a focused circular pool of light.
Motion & FX

5. High-Speed Golden Mist Burst

Volumetric spray mist with backlit microscopic droplets expanding dynamically into the dark air.

Blender Workflow:
  • Option A: Geometry Nodes particle emitter with cone dispersion.
  • Option B: Quick Liquid / Gas simulation (Mantaflow Smoke with high vorticity).
  • Motion Blur enabled (`Shutter: 0.5`) in Cycles render settings.
Typography & Branding

6. Gold Foil Serif Branding & Label

Refined high-fashion serif lettering with metallic debossing on textured matte charcoal paper.

Blender Workflow:
  • Design transparent label PNG with Alpha in Figma or Photoshop.
  • Mix Shader: Mix Glass BSDF with Gold Foil Metallic using Alpha as factor.
  • Add inverted Bump node into normal input for realistic debossed depth.
